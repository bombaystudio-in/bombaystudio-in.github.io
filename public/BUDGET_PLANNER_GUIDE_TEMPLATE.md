## 2026 Mumbai Interior Budget Planner & Architectural Checklist

### Executive Summary
This guide provides realistic rates, material specifications, and quality checklists for designing luxury residential apartments and turnkey villas in Mumbai (Bandra, Juhu, Worli, Lower Parel, Thane, Navi Mumbai & Alibaug).

---

### 1. Typical Turnkey Costs in Mumbai (per Carpet Area sq.ft.)
* **Standard Finish**: ₹1,350 – ₹1,500 / sq.ft.
  * *Commercial Plywood, 1mm Acrylic Laminate, Standard Soft-Close, OBD Paint.*
* **Premium Turnkey Finish**: ₹1,850 – ₹2,200 / sq.ft.
  * *Certified IS-710 BWP Marine Plywood, Matte PU Finish, Quartz Countertops, Hafele/Blum German Hardware.*
* **Ultra-Luxury Finish**: ₹2,800 – ₹4,000+ / sq.ft.
  * *Italian Botticino Marble, Natural Fluted Veneer, Architectural Magnetic Track Lighting, Smart Home Automation.*

---

### 2. Room-Wise Budget Breakdown Percentage
* **Living & Dining Room**: 30% of total budget (Flooring, Wall Veneer, Floating TV Console, Ambient Coves)
* **Modular Kitchen**: 25% of total budget (BWP Marine Ply, Blum Tandem Boxes, Quartz Counter, Hob & Chimney)
* **Master Bedroom & Wardrobes**: 25% of total budget (Floor-to-Ceiling Wardrobe, Headboard, Lighting)
* **Bathrooms & Utility**: 12% of total budget (8x4 Slab Tiles, Concealed Fittings, Vanity Quartz)
* **Electrical Rewiring & Painting**: 8% of total budget (Copper Concealed Wiring, ICA/PU Paint)

---

### 3. 15 Critical Questions to Ask Any Contractor Before Signing
1. Is the contract backed by a daily penalty clause for handover delays?
2. Which exact grade of plywood is specified (MR Grade vs IS-710 BWP Marine Ply)?
3. Are Blum / Hafele soft-close hardware fittings included in the itemized BOQ?
4. Is 3D visualization and lighting simulation included before execution starts?
5. Are daily site progress reports with photos provided via a dedicated Project Manager?
6. Does the quote include 100% complete civil cleanup and white-glove site deep cleaning?
