# Success Metrics

When the redesign is complete, the homepage should meet these goals:

- ✅ A visitor understands what Bombay Studio does within **5 seconds**.
- ✅ Every section communicates **one primary message**.
- ✅ The homepage copy is reduced by **at least 60%**.
- ✅ Project photography becomes the primary storytelling medium.
- ✅ The visual hierarchy naturally guides the eye from top to bottom.
- ✅ The website feels like a premium architecture and luxury lifestyle brand—not a brochure.
- ✅ Visitors are encouraged to **explore projects first**, then build trust, and finally book a consultation.

If executed well, this redesign would shift Bombay Studio from looking like a **feature-heavy agency website** to a **luxury brand experience**, similar to the digital presence of premium architecture firms and high-end lifestyle brands.
